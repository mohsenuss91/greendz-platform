## Data We Collect

We will only collect the minimum amount of data necessary for the platform to function. This will likely include:
*   **User Data:** Name, email, and Wilaya (province).
*   **Tree Data:** Location (latitude/longitude), photos, and status updates.

## How We Use Your Data

Your data will be used to:
*   Power the core features of the platform (e.g., display trees on the map).
*   Calculate community impact statistics.
*   Allow communication between the GreenDZ team and volunteers.

We will **never** sell your data to third parties.
