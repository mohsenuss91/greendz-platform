## What is GreenDZ?

GreenDZ is a web and mobile-first platform that connects volunteers with opportunities to make a real environmental impact. Through our interactive map, users can:
*   **Register** newly planted trees.
*   **Become a guardian** for existing trees, providing care and updates.
*   **Participate** in eco-challenges and earn badges.
*   **Track** our collective progress towards a greener Algeria.
